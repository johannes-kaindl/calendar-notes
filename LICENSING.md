# Licensing

Calendar & Contact Notes is **free software under the AGPL-3.0-or-later**
([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)), and it will stay that way. This file exists for the one case the
AGPL does not cover: if its copyleft does not fit your use case, a **commercial license is
available on request**.

## Which license applies to what

| Part | License |
| --- | --- |
| Code | [AGPL-3.0-or-later](LICENSE) |
| Third-party dependencies | see [`THIRD-PARTY.md`](THIRD-PARTY.md) |

## Asking for a commercial license

Write to **code@jkaindl.de** and describe what you want to build. Useful to include: how the
plugin (or parts of it) would be distributed, and whether your product is itself open source.
There is no price list — small and non-commercial uses are usually fine under the AGPL anyway,
and it is worth asking before assuming otherwise.

## Why this is possible — and what would end it

Dual-licensing only works while **one party holds the rights to all of the code**. As of
2026-08-23 that is the case: every commit in this repository is authored by Johannes Kaindl.
(AI-assisted commits carry a `Co-Authored-By` trailer for transparency; they are work made
under the author's direction and do not create a second rights holder.)

Should a third-party contribution ever be merged under a different arrangement, a commercial
license could no longer be granted for the project as a whole without that contributor's
agreement — and this file would be updated to say so. Until then the offer above stands
without asterisks.
